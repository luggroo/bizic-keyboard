The bundled model converts the released FP32 best.pt (SHA-256 151f32b03c49dbed77f3b8076035d28ce050d70a1a102e12581274a44cb10460) to ONNX with PyTorch 2.6.0, opset 14.
characters.txt is the source ncnn/charset.json in output-index order. Android preprocessing adapts the reference 360px pad and grayscale normalization.
Concatenate mobilenet-v2.onnx.0 and mobilenet-v2.onnx.1 in that order to recover the ONNX file.
